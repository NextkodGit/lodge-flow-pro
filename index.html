Steps — Opening
RWM: Brn., assist me to open this Lodge.
RWM: (to W.J.W.)—Br. A.B., what is the first duty of E.A.F.’s in Lodge assembled?
JW: To see that the Lodge is tyled.
RWM: You will direct that duty to be done.
JW: Bro. X., you will see that the Lodge is tyled.
IG: (The I.G. opens the door and sees that the Tyler is at his post.) I.G. (reports) Bro. A.B., the Lodge is tyled.
JW: The Lodge is tyled.
RWM: (to W.S.W.) Bro. X, what is the next duty?
SW: To see that none but F…s are present,
RWM: Are you satisfied that all present are F…s, and that they are properly clothed?
SW: I am.
RWM: To order, Brn., as E.A.Fs.
RWM: W.S.W., how many Office-bearers are necessary in the working of a Lodge?
SW: Seven..namely, the R.W.M., the S. and J.W…s, the S. and J.D…s, the I.G. and the O.G. or T.
RWM: W.J.W, the situation of the O.G.?
SW: Outside the door of the Lodge.
RWM: His duty?
SW: Being armed with a drawn sword, to keep off all cowans and eavesdroppers, and see that Candidates come properly prepared.
RWM: Brother I.G., your situation in the Lodge?
IG: Within the entrance of the Lodge.
RWM: Your duty?
IG: To admit Brethren on proof, receive Candidates in due form, and obey the commands of the W.J.W.
RWM: Br. J.D., your situation in the Lodge?
JD: At the right of, or near, the W.S.W.
RWM: Your duty?
JD: To carry all messages and communications of the R.W.M., from the W.S.W. to the W.J.W., and see the same punctually obeyed.
RWM: Br. S.D., your situation in the Lodge?
SD: At the right of, or near the R.W.M.
RWM: Your duty?
SD: To bear all messages and commands from the R.W.M. to the W.S.W., and to await the return of the J.D.
RWM: W.J.W., your constant place in the Lodge?
JW: In the S., R.W.M.
RWM: Why are you so placed?
JW: To mark the sun at its meridian, to call the Brethren from labour to refreshment, and from refreshment to labour again, that profit and pleasure may be the result.
RWM: W.S.W., your constant place in the Lodge?
SW: In the W., R.W.M.
RWM: Why are you so placed?
SW: To mark the setting sun, to close the Lodge at the R.W.M.’s command, after having seen that every Brother has had his due.
RWM: Bro. P.M., the Master’s place?
PM: In the E., R.W.M.
RWM: Why is he so placed?
PM: As the sun rises in the E. to open and enlighten the day, so is the R.W.M. placed in the E., to open the Lodge, and to employ and instruct the Brethren in Freemasonry
RWM: Brethren, the Lodge being thus duly constituted, before I declare it open, let us invoke a blessing from T.G.A.O.T.U. on all our undertakings.
RWM: Chaplain — May our labours begun in order, be conducted in peace and closed in harmony.
RWM: n the name of T.G.A.O.T.U., I declare this Lodge............ No............... on the roll of the Grand Lodge of Scotland duly opened for the purposes of Freemasonry in the First Degree.
